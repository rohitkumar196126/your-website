# SEO Checklist for Delish Bites

## 1) Before Going Live
- Buy a real domain and host on HTTPS.
- Replace `https://your-domain.com` in `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.
- Use absolute canonical URLs after your final domain is fixed.

## 2) Google Tools Setup
- Verify domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit `sitemap.xml` in Search Console.
- Keep your existing verification file: `google0a5520606bc8a37c.html`.
- Add your business details in Google Business Profile.

## 3) Content and Ranking Work
- Publish at least 1 new useful page weekly (offers, food guide, local delivery areas).
- Add real customer reviews/testimonials.
- Build local citations (Justdial, IndiaMART, social profiles, maps listings).
- Earn backlinks from local blogs, student communities, and partner sites.

## 4) Performance and UX
- Compress images to WebP where possible.
- Keep CLS low and ensure pages are mobile friendly.
- Track key events in Google Analytics (menu clicks, order submissions, calls).

## 5) Ongoing Monitoring
- Check Search Console weekly for indexing and coverage issues.
- Monitor top queries and improve pages with low CTR.
- Update page titles and descriptions based on real query data.
